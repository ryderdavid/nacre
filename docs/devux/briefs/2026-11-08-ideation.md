## What exists today (Nacre 0.44.0)

Repository: /Users/ryder/Code/Nacre. The app lives in `android/app/src/main/java/com/ryderdavid/nacre` (abbreviated A). nacred lives in `bridge/internal`.

- **New effort**
  - On an OpenSpec board, New effort starts a chat on the host. The chat's first message carries `DraftingPrompt`, defined in `bridge/internal/chat/drafts.go:31`. That prompt says:
    - talk the effort through and read the repo if it helps;
    - change nothing;
    - write a fenced `nacre-draft` JSON block (title, type, description, design, acceptance);
    - write a new block whenever the draft changes.
  - The app shows each block as a Draft card with Continue: `A/ui/chat/Conversation.kt`, `A/ui/workspace/ChatFirstShell.kt` and `WorkspaceScreen.kt`. Continue opens the New effort sheet and runs intake.
  - The draft-to-form code is in `A/data/chat/NewEffort.kt`.
- **Chat briefing** (`bridge/internal/chat/briefing.go`, about lines 40–110)
  - For new work, the agent drafts and raises an `intake` decision. It never creates beads or develops itself.
  - Mockups: the agent writes HTML or images to `$NACRE_MOCKUPS_DIR` and a `MEDIA:` line, and the phone renders them inline (0.42.0).
  - An "On screen:" note goes before every message.
- **Intake** (spec `openspec/specs/effort-intake/spec.md`)
  - One read-only call gives a verdict: just a bead, new change, amends <change>, or already covered. The verdict cites paths from the requirement index.
  - Ryder confirms or overrides it on a card or sheet.
  - Confirming a change creates the bead and requests development (the author and reviewer loop in `bridge/internal/specflow`). Proposals appear in the board's Proposals lane.
  - The pending-draft placeholder is uncommitted change `openspec/changes/pending-proposal-placeholders`.
- **Drafting loop** (spec `openspec/specs/spec-guided-work/spec.md`, plus active change `openspec/changes/drafting-settles-its-findings`)
  - The author and reviewer loop runs headless, up to five passes. The reviewer marks findings as fixable by the author or needing the owner.
  - The owner gets choices, then approves at a specific revision. Approval is a protected decision card.
- **Decision cards**
  - Kinds: approve, land, finish, signoff, limit, adopt, intake, found and question.
  - Specs: `openspec/changes/chat-first-harness/specs/owner-decisions/spec.md`, and `A/ui/workspace/decisions/DecisionCard.kt` and `DecisionModel.kt`.
  - Question cards (round questions, spec drift, intake) have choices with consequences, "In my own words", and "Ask about this", which explains and never answers.
  - The chat agent itself cannot raise a free-form multiple-choice question card while brainstorming. Its decision kinds are fixed (briefing.go).
- **Host tool prompts** (`bridge/internal/chat/permissions.go:86`)
  - Every `can_use_tool` request becomes a generic permission card (Allow/Deny with a summary). That includes AskUserQuestion and ExitPlanMode in the full harness.
  - The routed "quick answer" runs in plan mode. Its ExitPlanMode hands off to the full harness: `turn.go:~502`, `route.go`.
- **Chat UI** (`A/ui/workspace/chat/`: ChatPane, ChatTimeline, ChatDrawer, ThreadSwitcher, ChatModel)
  - Up to three composer suggestions, image attach, and a chats-and-jobs drawer with foldable chat cards.
  - Spec `openspec/specs/agent-chat/spec.md` and `chat-presentation`.
- **Needs you** (0.44.0): one check line, plus an in-order walkthrough of the waiting cards (`A/ui/workspace/decisions/Walkthrough.kt`, `A/ui/workspace/Interim.kt`).
- **Spec panel / change screen:** `A/ui/workspace/spec/`. **Beads panel:** `A/ui/workspace/beads/`.
