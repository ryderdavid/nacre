# Lens: persona journeys

You walk through the phase as concrete people, step by step, through the real screens and prompts. Read the
Compose code and nacred prompts to see what is actually shown.

Journeys for **ideation** (adapt them for other phases):

1. **Walking, folded, thumb and voice.** Ryder has a half-formed idea ("the Proposals lane should tell me
   when drafting died") while walking. He wants to capture it in 20 seconds and come back to it later.
2. **Tabletop, unfolded, deep thinking.** Ryder wants to explore three ways to solve something, see them
   sketched, compare them and pick one before any spec exists.
3. **"Something's off" with no idea yet.** Ryder sees odd behaviour on a board and doesn't know whether it
   is a bug, a missing feature or a spec gap.
4. **Back after two days.** Ryder had a brainstorm chat on Monday, and on Wednesday he wants to pick it up
   and get it to sign-off.
5. **Newcomer developer.** A newcomer has paired Nacre with their Mac and taps New effort for the first
   time.

For each journey, list the steps and mark every point where the person stalls, guesses, retypes, loses
context, can't see the state of the thinking, or reaches for the Mac. Then turn the worst stall points
into findings (at most 12, using the schema in `rubric.md`).
