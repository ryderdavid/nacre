# Lens: confusion recovery

You look at the moments when someone doesn't understand: Ryder, or the agent.

- **Ryder confused:**
  - jargon or terms he didn't choose (intake, amendment, covered, seed, round, adopt, found);
  - a card he doesn't know why he's getting;
  - a state he can't explain;
  - not knowing what tapping will do, or whether it can be undone.
- **The agent confused:**
  - it misreads him, drafts the wrong thing, or asks too many or too few questions;
  - he can't correct course without retyping everything.
- **Recovery tools:**
  - Ask about this;
  - In my own words;
  - undo or rewind;
  - "explain this screen";
  - a glossary;
  - the "On screen" context note;
  - how errors and failed drafts are worded.

For each one, check what exists, whether it can be found from where the confusion happens, and whether its
wording is plain. Return at most 12 findings, using the schema in `rubric.md`.
